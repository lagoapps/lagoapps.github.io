Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026

Lago Apps Group ("we", "our", or "us") operates mobile applications including Lago Mind and any future applications published under the Lago brand. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

Information We Collect

Our apps are designed with privacy in mind. Depending on how you use our applications, we may process the following types of information:

  • Data you enter directly into the app (such as financial records, notes, tasks, goals, or other personal content).
  • Basic device information such as operating system version and app version (only if required for app functionality or troubleshooting).

In general, Lago Apps Group applications are designed so that your personal data is stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Provide core app functionality
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Support future feature development

Data Storage

Users may choose to enter personal information into our applications, such as financial records, notes, tasks, goals, shopping lists, and other user-created content.

Your tasks, bills, lists, notes and other content you enter are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, upload, or store this content on Lago Apps Group servers. The only information that leaves your device because of the app itself is limited subscription information described under "Purchases and Subscriptions" and, if you use voice entry, the audio described under "Voice Entry".

If backup features are used, backup files are created and stored locally on your device. Lago Apps Group does not receive, access, or store backup files.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share limited information only with service providers needed to run the app's features: Google Play (payments and subscriptions), RevenueCat (verifying subscription status), and, if you use voice entry, the speech recognition service provided by your device (on Android, typically Google).

Because user data is stored locally on your device and is not collected by Lago Apps Group, we do not share personal data with third parties, advertisers, analytics providers, or marketing partners.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Voice entry uses the device speech recognizer. Voice entry is optional. Audio is processed by the speech recognition service on your device, which on Android may send it to Google to be turned into text. Lago Apps Group does not collect, store, or receive those recordings.

Notifications

Lago Mind may use local device notifications to remind users about bills, recurring tasks, shopping items, commitments, and other reminders configured by the user.

These notifications are generated and stored locally on the device. Lago Apps Group does not receive, collect, store, or transmit notification content.

Purchases and Subscriptions

> Lago Mind offers an optional paid subscription ("Lago Mind Premium"). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. > > To confirm whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase and subscription status (such as the product, purchase date, and expiry or renewal date), and basic device and app information needed to verify the purchase. RevenueCat does not receive the content you enter into the app. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. > > We do not use this information for advertising and we do not sell it. You can cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play (Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions).

Backups

Lago Mind may provide backup and restore functionality. Save Data writes an unencrypted JSON file to the folder you choose (usually Downloads/LagoMind). That file contains your financial records, tasks, lists and settings. Your app PIN is not included. Anyone, or any app, with access to that folder can read the file, so store or delete it carefully. Lago Apps Group does not receive backup files. Other apps or people who can read that folder can open it. Lago Apps Group does not receive backup files.

Lago Apps Group does not receive, upload, access, or store backup files.

Data Deletion

Users may delete their data at any time using the application's reset, deletion, backup management, or uninstall features.

Because user data is stored locally on the device, Lago Apps Group does not retain copies of user data after it has been deleted by the user.

Future Features

Lago Apps Group may introduce optional features in the future such as cloud synchronization, analytics, crash reporting, or subscription services. If such features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated before they are enabled.

Data Security

The app PIN is stored only on your device, in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore). It is not included in backups and is never sent to us.

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, since most data is stored locally on your device, security also depends on your device protection.

Children’s Privacy

Our applications are not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

Our apps do not include advertising or third-party analytics or tracking services. The subscription service described above is used only to verify purchases.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
