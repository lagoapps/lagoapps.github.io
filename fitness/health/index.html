Health disclaimer

Shown and accepted on first sign-in

Lago Fitness offers general fitness content. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor or physiotherapist.

  • Talk to your doctor before you start, especially if you are pregnant, injured, recovering from surgery, or have a heart, blood-pressure or other health condition.
  • Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
  • You exercise at your own risk and choose a level that suits you.
