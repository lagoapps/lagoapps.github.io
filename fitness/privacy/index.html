Privacy policy

Version 1.0

Lago Fitness is an app run by its owner in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Account: email, name, language, and the trainer you belong to.
  • Profile you choose to add: photo, age bracket (a 5-year range), height, weight, fitness level, focus zones and body type.
  • Activity: lessons watched, progress, goals and reminders.
  • Purchases: plan, store and status. Card details stay with Apple or Google.

Who sees it

Your trainer sees your name, email, plan and workout activity. Your body details (age bracket, height, weight, fitness level, focus zones and body type) are visible only to you. Your trainer and the app owner cannot see them.

Service providers

Supabase (data storage), VdoCipher (video), RevenueCat, Apple and Google (payments and subscriptions), and Firebase (notifications). Some providers store data outside Canada.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time. You can delete your account in Settings. We erase your data within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.
